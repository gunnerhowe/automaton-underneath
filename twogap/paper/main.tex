\begin{abstract}
Linear-RNN language models write into a fixed-size state on every token, and their two best-known
capability gaps --- associative recall and state tracking --- are both plausibly downstream of
undisciplined writes. The frontier now ships learned write gates (Gated DeltaNet-2), but no one has
reported what a learned write gate actually \emph{learns}, or whether write discipline causally buys
either capability during training. We pre-registered and ran a $13$-run controlled grid at
\tgParamsM{}M parameters ($\tgTrainTokensM$M tokens per run): gate arms
$\{$always-write, $L_1$-priced learned gate, unpriced learned gate, oracle gate$\}$
$\times$ tracking-data mixture $\{0,2,10,50\%\}$, on TinyStories plus synthetic state-tracking
families with token-level role annotations held out from every learned arm. Three results.
(1)~\emph{The price discovers hygiene}: with a small $L_1$ price on writing, the learned gate ends
training at mean $\tgHygOpBmFifty$ on state-\emph{operation} tokens versus $\tgHygOtherBmFifty$ on
content tokens --- recovering the oracle's write-suppression pattern with no access to role labels ---
while the unpriced gate stays $\tgHygOpRatioBpOverB\times$ more open on operations; the gap is
present at every mixture and grows with operation density. These are, to our knowledge, the first
token-role statistics of a learned write gate. (2)~\emph{Hygiene buys recall, cheaply but weakly}:
on pure language data the priced-gate arm beats always-write on multi-query recall by
$+\tgMqarDeltaSZero$/$+\tgMqarDeltaSOne$ across two seeds at matched perplexity (worst-case cost
$+\tgPplWorstCostPct\%$) --- directionally replicated, $2$--$3\times$ an identical-configuration
noise floor we measure directly, but below our pre-registered $+0.03$ margin at one seed, and from a
near-chance base. (3)~\emph{Hygiene does not buy automata}: the pre-registered
gating-helps-tracking-under-scarcity prediction died with the sign inverted, the only tracking skill
that emerged anywhere arose in the \emph{always-write} arm, and damping op-token writes at inference
\emph{destroys} that skill ($1.00\to\tgPfourDampToggleEight$) rather than revealing anything beneath
--- inverting the reveal effect our companion mechanism study established in cornered toy training,
and completing a three-regime scope map: write damping reveals hidden automata in minimal-width pure
training, repairs them in hybrid tasks, and deletes skills in LM training, where the write path is
how skills form. Finally, a zero-training, pre-registered follow-up measurement on a production
hybrid (Nemotron-Flash-3B, re-implemented torch-native) closes the scale question in both
directions: the production model's write knobs \emph{already} implement novelty-based hygiene ---
repeated facts are written more weakly than position-matched novel facts by $\tgProdDnDiff$
(deltanet $\beta$) and $\tgProdMtDiff$ (mamba2 $\Delta_t$), and our synthetic operation tokens are
write-suppressed out-of-distribution --- so the price is a small-scale scaffold for what
production-scale training discovers by default, and the surviving design rule is to \emph{measure}
hygiene, not necessarily to price it. All predictions, kills, and analysis rules were
pre-registered in-repository before any run; deviations are disclosed, and every number in this
paper regenerates from committed run artifacts.
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction}
A linear-RNN language model carries its entire context in a fixed-size matrix state, updated by a
write on every token. Its two most-cited capability gaps relative to attention --- associative
recall of stated facts \citep{arora2023zoology} and multi-step state tracking
\citep{merrill2024illusion,grazzi2025negative} --- have a shared candidate root: \emph{undisciplined
writing}. Every token overwrites state whether or not it carries information worth storing, so
stored facts get polluted (the recall gap), and state-transformation structure gets buried
(the tracking gap).

The field has begun to act on this intuition at the architecture level. Gated DeltaNet-2
\citep{hatamizadeh2026gdn2} decouples erase and write with channel-wise learned gates at 1.3B
parameters and reports better recall and perplexity; sparse-write designs improve recall in
expanded-state settings \citep{pan2025sse,xiao2026ramnet}; the lineage goes back through gated delta
rules \citep{yang2025gateddeltanet,yang2024deltanet,gu2023mamba} to learned update-skipping in
classical RNNs \citep{campos2018skiprnn}. But shipping a gate is not understanding one. To our
knowledge, no published work reports \emph{what a learned write gate learns} --- its statistics
against token roles --- nor whether write discipline \emph{causally} buys recall or tracking during
training, nor what it costs. Those are economics questions, and they are design-rule-relevant
precisely because the frontier is already paying for the machinery.

This paper asks the economics questions at garage scale, with pre-registered discipline. We train
\tgParamsM{}M-parameter DeltaNet-class language models from scratch under four write-gate arms ---
\textbf{A} always-write ($\gt\equiv 1$, the field default), \textbf{B} learned gate with an $L_1$
price on writing, \textbf{B$'$} learned gate unpriced (the Gated DeltaNet-2 species), and \textbf{C}
oracle gate (writes suppressed on ground-truth state-operation tokens) --- crossed with a
data-mixture axis $\{0,2,10,50\%\}$ of synthetic state-tracking text whose token roles
(content / operation / check) are annotated in the corpus but visible only to arm C. The synthetic
families sit at three algebraic tiers (memory-permutation $S_3$, involution $\mathbb{Z}_2$, cyclic
$\mathbb{Z}_3$), with evaluation chains up to $4\times$ deeper than training. Every prediction and
kill criterion was frozen in-repository before the first grid run.

\paragraph{Contributions.}
\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{The first token-role account of a learned write gate} (\S\ref{sec:hygiene}). Under an
$L_1$ price, the gate discovers write hygiene unsupervised: mean gate $\tgHygOpBmFifty$ on operation
tokens vs.\ $\tgHygOtherBmFifty$ on content at the 50\% mixture (gap $+\tgHygGapBmFifty$), with
$\tgBGateOffPct\%$ of gates effectively shut. The unpriced control gates operations at
$\tgHygOpBpmFifty$ --- $\tgHygOpRatioBpOverB\times$ more open --- and barely sparsifies
($\tgBpGateOffPct\%$ shut). Exploratory: the hygiene gap is dose-responsive in mixture
($+\tgHygGapBmTwo\to+\tgHygGapBmTen\to+\tgHygGapBmFifty$).
\item \textbf{A measured recall effect with honest error bars} (\S\ref{sec:recall}). Priced hygiene
lifts recall over always-write on pure LM data in both seeds run
($+\tgMqarDeltaSZero$, $+\tgMqarDeltaSOne$; matched perplexity), $2$--$3\times$ the
identical-configuration noise floor ($\tgNoiseFloorMqar$) that our own grid supplies, but below the
pre-registered $+0.03$ margin at seed~1 and from a near-chance base --- reported as supported and
fragile, not confirmed.
\item \textbf{A clean pre-registered negative on the tracking side} (\S\ref{sec:tracking}--\S\ref{sec:damping}).
Gating did not help tracking emerge anywhere on the grid; at 2\% scarcity the oracle arm was
\emph{worse} than always-write (predicted $>+0.10$, observed $\tgCminusADeepmTwo$). The one skill
that emerged --- perfect depth-$\le$8 toggle tracking, always-write arm, 50\% mixture --- lives
\emph{on} the write path: damping op-token writes at inference deletes it
($1.00\to\tgPfourDampToggleEight$ at depth 8) while doing exactly nothing
($\tgPfourDeepDeltamZero$) where no skill exists. With our companion mechanism study
\citep{howe2026automaton}, this completes a three-regime scope map for write-path intervention
(reveal / repair / destroy) whose boundary variable is how cornered training is.
\item \textbf{The production answer, measured not extrapolated} (\S\ref{sec:production}). A
separately pre-registered, zero-training measurement on NVIDIA's Nemotron-Flash-3B (weights
public; forward re-implemented torch-native and behaviorally validated) shows both of its write
knobs already suppress re-writes of known facts relative to position-matched novel facts
($\tgProdDnDiff$ / $\tgProdMtDiff$, both $2$--$3\times$ the frozen margin) and suppress our
operation tokens out-of-distribution --- production-scale training discovers hygiene without a
price, killing our own naive scaled proposal by its pre-registered go/no-go rule.
\item \textbf{Method disclosures the field can reuse}: an identical-computation noise floor hiding
inside any oracle-mask grid (\S\ref{sec:recall}); a constant-policy artifact that makes at-chance
accuracy tuples repeat across independent runs (\S\ref{sec:tracking}); and a pre-registered,
frozen-threshold emergence-forecasting protocol whose gate correctly declined to fire on this grid
(\S\ref{sec:fusion}).
\end{enumerate}

Everything here is \tgParamsM{}M-parameter scale on TinyStories-class data: we are validating and
killing \emph{design rules}, not making frontier claims. The rules that survive are candidates for
the next scale up; the rule that died, died cheaply.

\section{Related work}
\textbf{Write gating in linear attention.} Gated DeltaNet \citep{yang2025gateddeltanet} added a
scalar decay gate to the delta rule \citep{yang2024deltanet}; Gated DeltaNet-2
\citep{hatamizadeh2026gdn2} decouples erase from write with independent channel-wise sigmoid gates
and reports recall and perplexity gains at 1.3B --- establishing the machinery but reporting no gate
behavioral statistics and no causal isolation of the write side. Skip-RNN \citep{campos2018skiprnn}
learned binary state-update skipping in classical RNNs with a budget penalty --- the closest
historical relative of our priced gate --- but predates linear-attention states and reports skip
rates, not token-role alignment. Sparse-write memories \citep{pan2025sse,xiao2026ramnet} route
writes to state partitions for recall; none prices writing or measures learned-gate semantics.

\textbf{State tracking in linear RNNs.} Extended-eigenvalue and Householder-product designs give
DeltaNet-class models the expressivity for hard state tracking
\citep{grazzi2025negative,siems2025deltaproduct}; trained models still fail to length-generalize,
and recent work explicitly frames the gap as optimization, not expressivity
\citep{siems2026codestate,buitrago2025length,terzic2025pdssm}. \citet{siems2026codestate} show
always-write DeltaNet learns $S_5$ perfectly from pure synthetic REPL traces --- fixing the
undiluted regime as solved and moving the open question to \emph{diluted} streams, which motivated
our mixture axis. Our companion study \citep{howe2026automaton} gives a causal mechanism at toy
scale: the additive input path is a parasitic attractor that conceals learned automata, and damping
it at inference restores them. Whether that reveal effect survives real LM training is exactly this
paper's P4.

\textbf{Recall evaluation.} We use MQAR-style multi-query associative recall \citep{arora2023zoology}
rendered in our corpus format, TinyStories \citep{eldan2023tinystories} as the base LM distribution,
and report all chance levels inline.

\section{Setup}\label{sec:setup}
\textbf{Model.} A \tgParamsM{}M-parameter DeltaNet-class LM ($d{=}512$, 8 layers, 8 heads, tied
embeddings, 8k BPE): per head, state $S\in\mathbb{R}^{d_k\times d_v}$ updates by the delta rule with
$\beta_t\in[0,2]$ (negative-eigenvalue range \citep{grazzi2025negative}) and a scalar per-head write
gate $\gt$ on the \emph{value write only} (erase and routing untouched):
$S_t = S_{t-1}\!\left(I-\beta_t k_t k_t^{\top}\right) + \gt\,\beta_t\, v_t k_t^{\top}$.
Training uses a chunk-parallel WY formulation (pure PyTorch, chunk 128) verified equivalent to a
sequential reference to $2\times10^{-5}$ before any run. Runs are $\tgSteps$ steps
$\times$ $\tgTokensPerStep$ tokens $=$ \tgTrainTokensM{}M tokens, AdamW, bf16, one RTX 3080;
evaluation every $\tgEvalCadence$ steps with per-eval checkpoints retained.

\textbf{Arms.} \textbf{A}: $\gt\equiv1$. \textbf{B}: $\gt=\sigma(w_g^\top x_t)$ plus loss term
$\lambda\,\overline{|g|}$, $\lambda{=}3\times10^{-3}$ frozen from a pilot sweep. \textbf{B$'$}: same
learned gate, $\lambda{=}0$ (the Gated DeltaNet-2 species, scalar rather than channel-wise).
\textbf{C}: $\gt$ set by corpus annotations --- writes suppressed on operation tokens of synthetic
segments, free elsewhere; the causal upper bound on hygiene. Arms A/B/B$'$ never see annotations.

\textbf{Data.} TinyStories mixed with synthetic tracking text at mixture
$m\in\{0,2,10,50\%\}$: three families rendered as natural sentences ---
\emph{boxes} (3-object permutation tracking, $S_3$ tier), \emph{toggle} (one lamp,
$\mathbb{Z}_2$), \emph{dial} (mod-3 counter, $\mathbb{Z}_3$) --- with per-step state statements
making tracking directly loss-bearing, training chain depths 1--8, and token-level role spans
(content/operation/check) recorded in the shards. A generator self-test re-simulates 1{,}000
documents from raw text before any run.

\textbf{Evals and chance.} Tracking: held-out fixed-depth chains at depths $\{2,4,8,16,32\}$,
$n{=}50$ per family$\times$depth cell (binomial 95\% half-width $\approx0.14$ at $p{=}0.5$;
candidate-restricted scoring; chance $=0.5$ toggle, $1/3$ dial and boxes). Recall: MQAR
\citep{arora2023zoology} with 4/8/12 key--value pairs ($n{=}60$ each; aggregate chance
$\tgMqarChance$). LM quality: TinyStories validation perplexity. Gate behavior: mean $\gt$ and
fraction of gates $<0.1$, plus token-role statistics against the held-out oracle masks.

\textbf{Pre-registration.} Predictions P1--P6 and kills K1--K3 were frozen in-repository before any
grid run (commit \texttt{b868a75}); two amendments (batch-size/heartbeat infrastructure after a
silent-VRAM-spill false start; additive forecasting instrumentation) were committed before any
result existed. The frozen seed rule: any cell deciding a \emph{positive} verdict is replicated at a
second seed before the verdict is claimed; negatives stand on seed 0. The low-mixture (2\%) cells
were additionally held blind behind a separate forecasting freeze (\S\ref{sec:fusion}) and opened
only after that gate's verdict was committed. Deviations from the frozen plan are disclosed in
\S\ref{sec:limits}.

\section{Results}

\subsection{P2: the price discovers hygiene}\label{sec:hygiene}
The pre-registered headline bet: a small $L_1$ price on writing makes the learned gate (i) sparse
and (ii) \emph{role-aligned} --- quieter on state-operation tokens than on content --- without ever
seeing role labels. Both halves confirmed (Fig.~\ref{fig:hygiene}a). At the 50\% mixture the priced
gate ends training at mean $\tgBGateMean$ with $\tgBGateOffPct\%$ of gates below $0.1$; measured
against the held-out oracle masks, it averages $\tgHygOpBmFifty$ on operation tokens vs.\
$\tgHygOtherBmFifty$ on content/other tokens (gap $+\tgHygGapBmFifty$; $\tgHygNSeq$ training-format
sequences, $\tgHygNOpmFifty$ operation tokens). The unpriced control B$'$ --- same architecture,
same data, $\lambda{=}0$ --- ends at mean $\tgBpGateMean$ with only $\tgBpGateOffPct\%$ shut and
operation-token gate $\tgHygOpBpmFifty$: gating exists, hygiene is weak. The price is what buys the
role alignment, at a perplexity cost never exceeding $+\tgPplWorstCostPct\%$ (P6 passes; K3 never
fires).

Two robustness notes. First, gate behavior replicates: at $m{=}0$ the priced gate's final mean is
$\tgBGateMeanMZeroSZero$ vs.\ $\tgBGateMeanMZeroSOne$ across two seeds. Second (exploratory, not
pre-registered): the hygiene gap exists at \emph{every} mixture and grows with operation density ---
$+\tgHygGapBmTwo$ at 2\%, $+\tgHygGapBmTen$ at 10\%, $+\tgHygGapBmFifty$ at 50\% --- a
dose--response pattern consistent with the gate learning from the statistics of the operation tokens
themselves.

To our knowledge these are the first published token-role statistics of a learned write gate in any
linear-attention model. The measurement is model-agnostic --- it needs only gate activations and a
role-annotated probe corpus --- and applies directly to production gates (e.g.\ Gated DeltaNet-2's),
where whether trillion-token training discovers hygiene on its own is an open, now-testable
question.

\subsection{P3: hygiene buys recall --- small, replicated in direction, honestly fragile}\label{sec:recall}
The two-gap link prediction: on pure language data ($m{=}0$), the priced-gate arm beats always-write
on MQAR by $>+0.03$ at matched perplexity. Measured: $+\tgMqarDeltaSZero$ at seed 0 (pass) and
$+\tgMqarDeltaSOne$ at seed 1 (below margin), both at slightly \emph{better} perplexity
($\tgPplCostPctSZero\%$, $\tgPplCostPctSOne\%$), mean $+\tgMqarDeltaMean$
(Fig.~\ref{fig:hygiene}b). Under the frozen seed rule this is \textbf{supported directionally,
below margin at one of two seeds} --- a small, real-looking effect requiring more seeds, not a
confirmed $>3$-point claim.

Three calibrations the grid itself provides. (i)~\emph{Noise floor}: at $m{=}0$ the oracle mask is
all-ones, so arm C and arm A specify the same computation; their end-of-training difference ---
$|\Delta\text{MQAR}|=\tgNoiseFloorMqar$, $|\Delta\text{track}|=\tgNoiseFloorTrack$ --- is a direct
identical-configuration replicate floor (hardware nondeterminism plus one numerically-inert mask
multiply). The P3 effect is $2$--$3\times$ this floor at both seeds. (ii)~\emph{Chance framing}:
aggregate MQAR chance is $\tgMqarChance$; arm A sits essentially at it
($\tgMqarASZero$/$\tgMqarASOne$), so the honest statement is that hygiene lifts recall from
$\approx$chance to slightly above ($\tgMqarBSZero$/$\tgMqarBSOne$), and unevenly across load at
seed~0 ($\tgMqarBmZeroPFour$ vs.\ $\tgMqarAmZeroPFour$ at 4 pairs, $\tgMqarBmZeroPEight$ vs.\
$\tgMqarAmZeroPEight$ at 8, $\tgMqarBmZeroPTwelve$ vs.\ $\tgMqarAmZeroPTwelve$ at 12) --- absolute
recall at this scale is weak for every arm. (iii)~\emph{Exploratory cross-check}: the \emph{oracle} arm also out-recalls always-write
where it actually gates ($m{>}0$: $\tgCMqarmFifty$ vs.\ $\tgAMqarmFifty$ at 50\%,
$\tgCMqarmTen$ vs.\ $\tgAMqarmTen$ at 10\%), same sign as P3, not pre-registered.

\subsection{P1/P5: write discipline does not buy tracking --- sign inverted at scarcity}\label{sec:tracking}
The tracking-side hypothesis --- write discipline helps tracking emerge, most where tracking data is
rare --- died by its own pre-registered criteria. P1 predicted oracle-over-always-write deep-boxes
advantage $>+0.10$ at the 2\% mixture; measured $\tgCminusADeepmTwo$ --- sign inverted. P5 predicted
$C>A$ at every mixture; measured $\tgCminusADeepmTen$ at 10\%, $\tgCminusADeepmFifty$ at 50\%,
$\tgCminusADeepmTwo$ at 2\% --- mixed signs within a few noise floors. The fuller truth visible in
Table~\ref{tab:grid}: \emph{deep boxes never emerged in any arm} --- all thirteen runs sit in a
chance band around $1/3$ --- so the P1/P5 contrasts are movements inside noise on a task nobody
learned. Gating didn't rescue deep tracking; nothing did, at this scale and budget.

Two honest instrument notes. First, at-chance cells collapse to \emph{constant answer policies}:
a model that always answers ``on'' scores exactly the eval pack's gold-label frequency at each
depth, which is why identical accuracy tuples recur across independent runs in
Table~\ref{tab:grid} --- repeated values are a fingerprint of at-chance behavior, not copied
evaluations. Second, dial-8 sits in $[\tgDialEightMin,\tgDialEightMax]$ (chance $\tgDialChance$) in
\emph{every} run trained on dial data --- the fourth consecutive experimental generation in this
program (toy grids, 5-seed note grids, and now every LM arm) in which a cyclic mod-3 task drifts
while same-tier non-cyclic structure locks. We leave the abelian-drift wall as a named open problem;
our own earlier mechanistic hypothesis for it was killed in \citet{howe2026automaton}.

\subsection{The emergence event that did happen --- and what it is not}\label{sec:emergence}
Exactly one tracking skill emerged on the grid: the \emph{always-write} arm at the 50\% mixture
reached perfect toggle accuracy at depths 2/4/8
($\tgAmFiftyToggleTwo$/$\tgAmFiftyToggleFour$/$\tgAmFiftyToggleEight$), already $\ge0.9$ by the
first evaluation ($\tgEmergeStepK$k steps) and perfect at every later one except a single transient
reversion at $\tgToggleDipStepK$k steps, while every gated arm --- priced, unpriced, oracle ---
stayed at chance ($\tgOthersToggleEight$) all the way to \tgTrainTokensM{}M tokens
(Fig.~\ref{fig:emergence}). Write gating didn't merely fail to help the easiest family; every form
of it \emph{prevented} the one emergence the always-write baseline found instantly.

Honesty requires deflating the event itself: the skill is depth-bounded, not an automaton. Accuracy
falls to $\tgAmFiftyToggleSixteen$/$\tgAmFiftyToggleThirtyTwo$ at depths 16/32 --- inside the
training envelope it is perfect, one step beyond it is chance. Even the grid's sole winner is
pattern mastery within the trained depth range, consistent with the unexplored-states account of
recurrent length generalization \citep{buitrago2025length}, and \emph{not} the length-generalizing
``automaton underneath'' of the toy regime \citep{howe2026automaton}.

\subsection{P4: damping destroys --- the reveal effect inverts at LM scale}\label{sec:damping}
Our companion study established, in cornered toy training, that damping the additive input path at
inference \emph{reveals} a correctly-learned automaton hiding under a parasitic shortcut
\citep{howe2026automaton}. P4 pre-registered the same intervention here: damp op-token value-writes
on trained always-write checkpoints; predict improvement at $m{=}50$ (where tracking data forced a
skill) and nothing at $m{=}0$.

Measured (Fig.~\ref{fig:damping}): the $m{=}0$ half held exactly ($\tgPfourDeepDeltamZero$
composite change --- the intervention is a true no-op where no skill exists), and the $m{=}50$ half
\emph{inverted}: damping op-token writes destroys the toggle mastery ---
$\tgPfourBaseToggleTwo\to\tgPfourDampToggleTwo$ at depth 2,
$\tgPfourBaseToggleFour\to\tgPfourDampToggleFour$ at depth 4,
$\tgPfourBaseToggleEight\to\tgPfourDampToggleEight$ at depth 8; composite
$\tgPfourDeepDeltamFifty$. There is nothing underneath to reveal: the skill \emph{lives on} the
write path.

Combined with the toy and hybrid results, the program's mechanism now has a complete,
pre-registered scope map:

\begin{center}\small
\begin{tabular}{lll}
\toprule
training regime & write-path damping does & evidence \\
\midrule
minimal-width, pure tasks (cornered) & \textbf{reveals} hidden automaton & \citet{howe2026automaton} \\
hybrid content+tracking tasks & \textbf{repairs} damaged solutions & \citet{howe2026automaton}, hybrid bridge \\
LM training (this paper) & \textbf{destroys} the formed skill & P4, \S\ref{sec:damping} \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

The boundary variable is how cornered training is. When the loss admits only the automaton, writes
are parasitic and damping strips the parasite. When training is a language-modeling problem,
\emph{writing is how the skill forms and where it lives}: the same intervention that exposes hidden
structure in the toy deletes real structure in the LM. Any at-scale proposal built on the toy
result's ``suppress the writes'' intuition inherits this boundary --- which is exactly why the
cheap experiment was worth running before anyone runs the expensive one.

\subsection{The pre-registered forecasting gate correctly declined to fire}\label{sec:fusion}
This grid double-served as the test bed for a frozen emergence-forecasting protocol (dense probe
plus behavioral anchors every 500 steps, composed-anchor event definition, thresholds
$(\tgFusionAnchorTp,\tgFusionAnchorTb)$ frozen before any grid value was read, low-mixture cells
held blind). Its own kill criterion fired: $\tgFusionNEvent/7$ calibration runs reached the frozen
composite event bar --- mean(toggle-8, dial-8) $\ge0.9$ sustained --- because the bar's dial half
never emerged anywhere (\S\ref{sec:tracking}), and its toggle half, where it emerged at all, was
already mastered by the first evaluation ($\tgFusionNearMissToggle$ toggle vs.\
$\tgFusionNearMissDial$ dial at $m{=}50$). A third, independent blocker: the representation-probe
anchor itself carried no signal at this scale --- its maximum over the entire A@m50 run was
$\tgProbeMaxAmFifty$, far below the frozen $\tgFusionAnchorTp$ firing threshold
(Fig.~\ref{fig:emergence}b) --- so the composed anchor could not have fired even had an approach
window existed. Nothing was forecastable at this scale and cadence: one target never arrives, the
other arrives before the first measurement, and the leading indicator that worked in the toy regime
is mute here. The protocol's verdict
was committed \emph{before} the blind cells were opened, no threshold was fitted post hoc, and any
future relaxed-bar analysis of these artifacts is pre-labeled exploratory in the registration.
We report this as the protocol working as designed --- a frozen gate that declines to fire on
inadequate data is the failure mode you want.

\subsection{The production answer: hygiene emerges without a price at 2.75B}\label{sec:production}
Our hygiene statistic transfers as-is to any checkpoint exposing per-token write knobs, which
turned the scale objection to this paper --- ``at production scale the gate learns hygiene anyway''
--- into a measurement rather than a debate. We pre-registered it separately (knobs named from
static code inspection before any forward pass; thresholds and a position-controlled probe frozen;
go/no-go rule fixed) and ran it on \textbf{Nemotron-Flash-3B}, NVIDIA's evolutionary-searched
production hybrid (6 DeltaNet layers with $\beta_t=\sigma(w_b^\top x_t)$ write strengths, 9 Mamba2
layers with $\Delta_t$ integration strengths, weights public). Windows has no triton, so the model
was re-implemented torch-native from its shipped source (sequential fp32 recurrences, all 231
tensors strict-loaded) and behaviorally validated before any knob was read (TinyStories NLL
$\tgProdNll$; coherent factual continuations).

The primary probe: sequences of 8 facts followed by the \emph{same} 8 facts restated, against a
position control of 16 distinct facts. Both knob species write repeated facts substantially more
weakly than position-matched novel facts --- deltanet $\beta$: $\tgProdDnRep$ vs.\
$\tgProdDnCtl$, difference $\tgProdDnDiff$ (95\% CI $[\tgProdDnCiLo,\tgProdDnCiHi]$); mamba2
$\Delta_t$: $\tgProdMtRep$ vs.\ $\tgProdMtCtl$, difference $\tgProdMtDiff$
(CI $[\tgProdMtCiLo,\tgProdMtCiHi]$) --- both clearing the frozen $-0.05$ margin by $2$--$3\times$
(Fig.~\ref{fig:production}a). And on our own synthetic families --- text this model never trained
on --- both knobs suppress operation tokens relative to content in all three families
(deltanet $\tgProdSThreeDnOp$ vs.\ $\tgProdSThreeDnOther$; mamba2 $\tgProdSThreeMtOp$ vs.\
$\tgProdSThreeMtOther$; Fig.~\ref{fig:production}b): the signature our priced 30M gate had to be
paid to learn appears out-of-distribution in a production model, for free. Two scoping facts: the
knobs are \emph{not} lexical-class trackers (content-vs-function-word gaps $\tgProdSTwoDnGap$ /
$\tgProdSTwoMtGap$ --- novelty- and role-based, not part-of-speech), and hygiene requires depth
(each species' first layer shows no repeat-vs-control contrast: $\tgProdDnLZeroRep$ vs.\
$\tgProdDnLZeroCtl$).

By the pre-registered go/no-go rule this is a NO-GO for the naive scaled proposal: production-scale
training discovers recall-side write hygiene without a price. The completed arc is two-sided ---
\textbf{hygiene must be bought at 30M scale and emerges by default at 2.75B} --- and to our
knowledge this is the first behavioral account of a production model's write gates anywhere.

\section{Discussion}\label{sec:discussion}
\textbf{For designers of gated linear-attention models.} Three design-relevant facts, each
pre-registered or disclosed exploratory. (1)~A scalar $L_1$ price turns a write gate into a
\emph{hygiene} mechanism --- role-aligned write suppression discovered from task statistics alone
--- at sub-1\% perplexity cost; unpriced gates (the shipped default) leave most of that alignment
on the table ($\tgHygOpRatioBpOverB\times$ weaker operation-suppression at matched architecture).
(2)~The payoff of hygiene, where we can measure it, is on the \emph{memory} side (recall), not the
\emph{computation} side (tracking): priced hygiene lifted near-chance recall in both seeds at
matched perplexity, while no form of write discipline --- learned, priced, or oracle --- helped any
tracking family emerge. (3)~Write freedom during skill formation matters: the only arm that
mastered any tracking was the one allowed to write everywhere, and its skill demonstrably lives on
those writes. A gate schedule that prices writes for state cleanliness \emph{after} skills form is
a different (untested) proposal than gating throughout training --- our results caution against the
latter and say nothing yet about the former.

\textbf{The measurable question, measured.} Our hygiene statistic transfers as-is to production
checkpoints, and \S\ref{sec:production} ran it: production-scale training discovers recall-side
hygiene without a price. The design guidance therefore inverts from ``price the gate at scale'' to
``\emph{measure} the gate at scale'' --- the statistic is a zero-training diagnostic of whether a
given checkpoint's write machinery has learned novelty discipline, and (open question) whether its
per-checkpoint value predicts recall quality across models.

\textbf{The two-gap hypothesis, split.} This program began from the intuition that recall pollution
and tracking failure share the undisciplined-write root. The verdict is a clean split: the recall
half survives (hygiene helps, a little, for free), the tracking half dies at LM scale (writes are
load-bearing for skill formation), and the toy-regime reveal effect that motivated the tracking
half is now scope-bounded to cornered training. ``The gate learns hygiene; hygiene buys memory,
not automata.''

\section{Limitations and disclosures}\label{sec:limits}
\textbf{Scale and power.} \tgParamsM{}M parameters, TinyStories-class data, $S_3$-tier synthetic
tracking, $n{=}50$ per tracking cell ($\pm\approx0.14$), MQAR from a near-chance base, single seed
except the pre-registered $m{=}0$ replication pair. The recall effect specifically needs more
seeds before any $>$noise-floor-multiple claim hardens. Gate parameterization is per-head scalar;
Gated DeltaNet-2's is channel-wise --- our economics conclusions are parameterization-scoped. The
production measurement (\S\ref{sec:production}) is one model at one snapshot, through a
torch-native re-implementation validated behaviorally rather than kernel-bit-exactly, with a
narrow first-vs-repeat template and a cc-by-nc research-use license; its synthetic-role slice is
out-of-distribution by design, where a positive is informative but a null would have been
ambiguous.

\textbf{Deviations from the frozen plan, disclosed.} (i)~The pre-registered $n_h{=}2$ extension
pair was not run: the program's decision questions resolved at $n_h{=}1$ and the budget moved to
the seed replication. (ii)~The frozen contingency (state-passing fine-tune \citep{buitrago2025length}
before any write-path attribution, triggered ``if the $m{=}50$ A arm fails deep tracking'') was not
run: A@m50 mastered the toggle family P4 measures while deep \emph{boxes} emerged in no arm
(\S\ref{sec:tracking}), and we judged the trigger's intent --- don't attribute a \emph{failure} to
write behavior without the rescue control --- inapplicable to P4's actual finding, which attributes
a \emph{success} to the write path. Readers may weigh that judgment; the fine-tune remains the
right control for any future deep-boxes attribution. (iii)~Training shards were accidentally
committed early in the program, silently blocking pushes; the unpushed range was rewritten to
excise the data files (content otherwise unchanged), so the pre-registration commits carry
third-party timestamps only from the day the block was found, with ordering enforced by the
intra-repository hash chain. Old$\to$new hashes are mapped in the repository's
\texttt{VERDICTS.md}. (iv)~The role-annotated shards were deleted from disk in cleanup; the
hygiene measurement (\S\ref{sec:hygiene}) runs on a deterministic regeneration of each training
corpus prefix (seeded generators, frozen tokenizer), committed as \texttt{hygiene.json}; it
reproduces the originally-logged values within sampling noise. (v)~The forecasting protocol's
richer analyses (interval calibration, anchor-lead distributions) never activated because zero
events occurred; only the gate verdict is claimed.
